Our Journey

Two Decades of Impact

From a bold vision to a nationwide legacy — the First Fiddle story is one of passion, perseverance, and perpetual reinvention.

2002

The First Note

Founded in Delhi with a single flagship property — a bold bet on experiential dining in India's nascent F&B landscape.

2006

Dragonfly Takes Flight

Launch of Dragonfly — India's first luxury rooftop Pan-Asian experience, instantly becoming Delhi's most coveted address.

2010

Portfolio Expansion

Bougie, Lord of the Drinks, and Warehouse Café added to the portfolio, cementing First Fiddle as a multi-concept powerhouse.

2015

National Recognition

Multiple national awards for innovation, ambience and guest satisfaction — setting benchmarks for the entire industry.

2020

Resilience & Reinvention

Navigated the pandemic with agility — pivoting to curated delivery, cloud kitchens and virtual dining without compromising quality.

2024+

The Next Chapter

Aggressive expansion into Tier-2 cities, franchise scale-up, and two new brand concepts to capture the next generation of diners.
